# Permissions

Social Poster's permissions cover its own section of the control panel, where you connect social media accounts and review what's been posted. Set them in **Settings → Users → User Groups**, or on an individual user's **Permissions** tab.

**Access Social Poster** is listed under **General**, with Craft's other permissions for reaching each plugin's section. The other three are listed under the **Social Poster** heading.

| Permission | What It Allows |
| --- | --- |
| Access Social Poster | Reach the Social Poster section of the control panel. |
| Posts | Open **Posts** to see every post sent to a social media account, with its response, and re-post one. Also allows deleting posts from that list, and choosing them in element selectors. |
| Accounts | Open **Accounts** to create, edit, reorder and delete [accounts](docs:feature-tour/accounts), enter each provider's credentials, and connect or disconnect the social media account through OAuth. |
| Settings | Nothing at present. Social Poster's settings are only available to admins, whether or not someone has this permission. |

**Posts** and **Accounts** work inside the plugin's section, so anyone given either needs **Access Social Poster** too. Each one adds its own item to the section's navigation, and the two are independent. Admins have every permission.

The [entry widget](docs:feature-tour/entry-widget) isn't controlled by these permissions. Anyone who can save an entry in a section that shows the widget can post it to your enabled accounts by saving it live, so use Craft's section permissions to decide who can do that.

Re-posting sends the post to the social media account again straight away, and **Accounts** lets someone connect a different account or disconnect one, so keep both for the people who look after your social media.
